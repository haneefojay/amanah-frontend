# Using the installed design skills on Amanah

Several third-party design skills are installed in `.claude/skills/`. They overlap and some pull in opposite directions. This guide says which one to reach for, and how Amanah's rules constrain them.

## Precedence

1. **Amanah rules** (this skill, `CLAUDE.md`, the spec) — always win.
2. **Amanah design truth** — tokens in `src/app/globals.css` and, once F1.1 lands, `DESIGN.md` at the repo root. Design skills must _extend_ these tokens, never fork a parallel palette, type scale or easing set.
3. **The skill for the job** (table below).
4. General taste from any other skill.

If a skill's instructions conflict with 1 or 2 (e.g. it wants a different palette, web fonts, decorative stock imagery, or a dashboard-hostile layout), follow Amanah and mention the conflict to the user. A new visual direction is a design decision: propose it, get approval, record an ADR.

## Product context to give any design skill

- **Product:** transparent donation platform for verified Islamic organisations in Nigeria. Trust, dignity, clarity. Not flashy fintech, not a charity guilt-trip.
- **Users:** donors on mid-range Android phones over slow networks; organisation admins and witnesses doing tasks in the field (often outdoors, one-handed); platform admins at a desk.
- **Surfaces and modes:** public campaign list and campaign/accountability pages = mostly _Read_ with a clear _Persuade_ moment (donate); dashboards (org, witness, admin) = _Operate_. The home hero is the only true marketing surface.
- **Constraints:** mobile first (360px), system fonts, tiny JS budget (F13.3), WCAG AA, offline-aware, `prefers-reduced-motion`, real campaign photos supplied by organisations (never stock imagery pretending to be evidence).
- `PROJECT.md` is the product brief; spec at `docs/amanah-mvp-spec.md`.

**frontend-design** (Anthropic, official) is installed in the project too. Use it for the design _plan_ of a new surface (token proposal, type, layout sketch, then a review against the brief) and for its UX-writing guidance, which suits Amanah's copy (plain verbs, sentence case, errors that say what happened and how to fix it). Two adjustments for Amanah: its "choose distinctive typefaces" advice gives way to the system-font rule (slow networks), and any new palette it proposes is a proposal for approval, not a replacement for the tokens in `globals.css`.

## Definition of Done for design work

In addition to the roadmap DoD: checked at 360px and desktop in a real browser; empty/loading/error/worst-case states reviewed (break-ui); contrast AA; no new raw hex; motion respects reduced-motion; screenshots attached to the PR for visual changes.
