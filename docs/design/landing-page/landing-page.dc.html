Generosity runs on trust. Trust needs proof.

Most giving ends at the payment. Donors send money and hope it arrives. Organisations doing honest work have no simple way to show where it went.

Amanah turns every campaign into an open record: what was needed, what was given, what was bought, and who saw it delivered. Nothing to take on faith.

From need to delivery, in the open

Five steps, each one visible on the campaign page. If a step is missing, everyone can see it.

  1. 1

    Verified first

    The Amanah team checks every organisation before it can publish a single campaign.

  2. 2

    Itemised needs

    Each campaign lists exactly what it needs, item by item, with a fixed target that adds up to the naira.

  3. 3

    You give

    Pay securely through Paystack. Your amount is held for you while you pay, so the target is never overshot.

  4. 4

    Spending, with proof

    Every purchase is logged with its receipt and photos taken in the app, stamped with time and place.

  5. 5

    A witness confirms

    A respected community member, independent of the organisation, confirms each delivery, or disputes it in public.

Never a kobo beyond the target.

When a campaign is fully funded, it closes itself. No organisation on Amanah can receive more than it asked for. The rule is enforced by the system, not left to good intentions.

  • Targets are fixed before a campaign goes live, and locked after the first gift.
  • Two donors paying for the last ₦100,000 at the same moment cannot push it over. Amounts are reserved before payment.
  • If a late payment ever slips past, the excess is flagged and returned to the donor, or passed to Bayt al-Mal where return is not possible.
